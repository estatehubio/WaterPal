Questions

Before You Book Your Water Test

Straight answers to what homeowners ask us most.

Is the water test really free?
Yes. We test your water in your home at no cost and with no obligation to buy.
Do I need a whole-house system or just a drinking water filter?
It depends on your test results. Whole-house systems treat water at every tap and shower. Reverse osmosis is a dedicated system for drinking and cooking water. Many homes use both.
Do you treat well water?
Yes. Well water often needs different treatment than city water, so we test for the issues private wells commonly have.
How much does a water filtration system cost?
Every system is tailored to your property and the services it needs. You’ll get a clear quote after your free water test.
Can I keep my existing water softener?
Often, yes. We check it during your water test and only suggest replacing it if it isn’t doing the job.
How long does installation take?
Install time depends on the system. We’ll confirm the timeline with your quote.
What does the water test check for?
We check the common issues in local homes, like hardness, chlorine, iron and pH, then explain what each result means for you.
Will a filtration system lower my water pressure?
We size every system to your home’s water flow, so you keep the pressure you’re used to.
How often do filters need to be changed?
It depends on the system and your water. We set a schedule for your home and remind you when it’s time.
